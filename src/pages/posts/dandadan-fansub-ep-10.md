> 妻もこれと同じ病気でなくなりました。治療には𓉓𓁻𓃅𓄲

> My wife died of the same disease. For treatment, I need 𓉓𓁻𓃅𓄲

I have been making some decisions on how I subtitle things based on how the manga presents the text. For example, using the scary font for lines that the manga also used a spooky font for. When I rewatched this episode for my translation, and I got to this gag where the Mantisian keeps saying words the humans can’t understand, I immediately went to the manga to see how they handled the same gag in written word.

![A black and white manga panel of Momo cupping her ear, with a speech bubble full of alien glyphs and another of her saying she can't make out what was said.](/images/posts/dandadan-fansub-ep-10/image-2.png)

As you can see, they used some made-up script that’s very similar to Egyptian hieroglyphics, which I’m guessing is a mini-reference to how aliens are associated with ancient Egypt. And since Unicode does in fact have hieroglyph support, I was able to recreate the gag reasonably faithfully!

![Okarun grits his teeth and clenches his fists at the figure in the pink polka-dot robe, with Seiko standing behind him. The subtitle reads "It's 2024!"](/images/posts/dandadan-fansub-ep-10/image-3.png)

> Seiko: 待てよ。おめえの血よ、くせえんだよ。 \
> Okarun: ちょっと！なんてこというんですか！宇宙人差別だ！ポリコレ！

> Seiko: Hold it. Your blood is rank as hell. \
> Okarun: Hey! You can’t say that! That’s alienphobic! It’s 2024!

I was really happy with my translation of this line! I wanted to emphasise the comedic timing of the joke, so I chose to cut Okarun’s line into multiple separate subs. I think quick cuts between short sub lines lead to better comedy because the audience can’t read ahead to the next part of the joke too soon, and also so that the delivery of each section is tightly paired with each sub.

In this case, the 3 subs are:

> ちょっと！なんてこというんですか！\
> Hey! You can’t say that!

> 宇宙人差別だ！\
> That’s alienphobic!

> ポリコレ！\
> It’s 2024!

The first two are reasonably straightforward (though it does tickle me seeing Okarun spout stereotypical SJW phrases in defence of an alien), but the final one is a more radical translation. The Japanese is “pori-kore!”, an abbreviation of “political correctness”, and one issue with it is that it’s so damn brief that I needed something that was around 10 letters or less for it to work as a readable sub in the time it’s on screen. The other issue is that it’s not a thing in English to claim “political correctness!”. The best translation of this line would be something comically cliched that people say when calling out bigotry, and so I settled on “It’s 2024!”
