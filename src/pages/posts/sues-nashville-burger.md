I love a Nashville burger. The very first one I ever had was at Blute’s almost 9 years ago when they offered it as a secret menu item[^1]. The combination of rich, dripping lard and colon-crushingly copious amounts of cayenne sold me on the recipe for life.

However, I was disappointed when this one arrived at the table. It was neatly assembled; a single stingy piece of fried chicken, without much visible spice seasoning constituted my primary dissatisfaction. Sue’s has fantastic smash burgers, so my expectations were much higher than perhaps they should have been.

But in a twist of fate, this burger was actually very good! It was not a traditional Nashville given its lower spice content and over reliance on the additional sauce spread on the bun (rather than on the cayenne coating), but it was delicious. Despite its small size and seeming thinness, the half-thigh (I assume) chicken patty was in fact perfectly cooked and juicy, and the coating was tastier than I estimated it to be on first glance.

I would happily get it again. Though I would try not to forget to order a side given that it is a little small.

3/4

[^1]: Unfortunately due to a long standing grudge that my beloved partner bears towards Blute’s I can’t in good conscience recommend that establishment
