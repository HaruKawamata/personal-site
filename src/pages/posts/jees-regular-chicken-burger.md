Jee’s Burgers used to be my go to burger place. Since my office moved further into the Valley, it’s been a slightly longer trek, and a little harder to justify the walk. However, I went back for the first time in a while recently and I was not disappointed! It was exactly as I remembered it from 3 or so years ago, so you can rest assured they are consistent if nothing else.

I got my old order, which was a “regular” fried chicken burger. When I made the order, the guy said “just so you know, regular means two patties,” and I just nodded knowingly. I know what I’m about. They do have a “small” fried chicken burger, which is one piece of fried chicken and what I would consider a normal sized burger. The regular is kind of obscene in comparison. You need to unhinge your jaw to eat it. The box can't close around it so they put a skewer through the half-shut lid of the box and through the burger. I know a few coworkers who used to take the second piece of chicken off and eat it as an afternoon snack.

The texture was really good. The chicken and cabbage both have a nice satisfying crunch in their own ways, and the buns are nice and soft. Yet I found myself, as always, wanting more flavour. The chicken tastes like fried chicken, and is very juicy, but there’s not much else going on. There is a bit of burger sauce, but I honestly think they could double the amount and the burger would be much better. Though despite the lack of sauce, it didn’t feel dry at all. I might try to remember to pay extra for some extra sauce or jalapenos next time.

Speaking of cost, I don’t usually think price worth remarking upon since most burger places have a similar pricing scheme, but the Jee’s regular chicken burger is only $15! An absolute steal.

3/4
