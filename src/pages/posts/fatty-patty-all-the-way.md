When I started these reviews I had the guideline that a 4/4 would be a meal I’d make a special effort to go back and eat again. I usually like to try a new thing each time I go back to a restaurant, so it’s a tough order to fill. But I found myself doing just that with Fatty Patty. Last time I got a double All The Way, and on my return trip I couldn’t stop myself from ordering the exact same thing a second time.

First things first — the smash patties are the best I’ve had, ever. They’re smashed paper thin and have an insanely crispy and flavourful lace on the edges, which results in a beautiful heterogeneity between bites. The middle bites are soft and have more variety of flavour, while the edge bites are an explosion of Maillard crunch.

The burger sauce was not too sweet, and there was a good amount of it to lubricate the crisped up patties. The tomato and lettuce also provided much needed moisture. I think people underestimate the importance of moisture in a burger or sandwich. It’s very important to the eating experience, hence why I place such importance on tomatoes and lettuce being present in a burger. Because if you’re making a plain cheeseburger with no salad, you have an uphill battle for moisture. You need to have some combination of very juicy patties, liberally buttered buns, and a lot more sauce than usual.

The buns were very soft and nicely toasted. They were the perfect medium for soaking up the beef fat and other drippings. The pickles also worked very well. They were sour, rather than sweet or excessively salty, and it’s just what this very greasy, very salty burger needed.

This is close to my ideal burger. I’ll be back, and I may even get the same thing a third time (or even add a third patty!)

4/4
