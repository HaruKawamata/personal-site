# Warzoid (Float form) / グンカンムリ（浮袋姿）

It is completely blind. It’s not very efficient, but it catches food with its sticky, pink organ. It’s highly intelligent, but it’s just a floating head so there’s nothing it can do about its situation. Floating on the surface of the ocean, it searches for Flail form Warzoids.

# Warzoid (Flail form) / グンカンムリ（毒針姿）

It’s hopeless at swimming. Despite the fact it’s a predator, catching food is so difficult that they often die of starvation. When they do catch prey, they firmly grasp it in their fist while dissolving it in acid, then absorb the nutrients through the skin. Though it looks completely different to Float form Warzoids, they are the same species, with the exact same DNA.

# Crown o’ War / スイソウノウエボシ

It’s often treated as a separate lifeform, but it’s actually just four Flail form Warzoids attached to a single Float form Warzoid. The Flail Warzoids have burrowed their way through the soft pink organ. With its 4 thick stingers it swims at high speeds, it’s very intelligent, and has sharp vision. Because of this, it’s also known as the “Terror of the Seas”.
