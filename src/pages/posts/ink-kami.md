Ink kami move into the pens and pencilcases of people who write and draw often. Like most kami, they encourage behaviour that they like by heightening the positive mental effects of it. Ink kami enjoy seeing creativity at work, so they are said to make the satisfying feeling of writing or drawing stronger. Recently, they have also been known to make tablet pens and laptop stickers their homes.
