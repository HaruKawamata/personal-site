# Hikikonoko / ヒキコノコ

Its true fungal body stays hidden inside its block of compost and never leaves. It has a particular dislike of humans.

# Hirakitake / ヒラキタケ

Now that it’s fully grown, it has come out of its shell. It’s extremely friendly, so it often tries to hug strangers. If it hugs you, you’ll be covered in dirt.
