This was my second trip to Barbecue Mafia, and I had high hopes. On my last trip I had something like a cheesesteak smash burger, which was insanely good. This time I ordered the pastrami double smash.

At $25 with chips, this monstrous burger is an insane deal. Look at the sheer amount of meat! It has two smash patties and a mountain of pastrami. The pickles and saurkraut struggle valiantly to balance the flavours with some tart acidity, but it's all in vain. Everything is drowned out by the meat.

The pastrami is really very good, and the smash patties were also great, but it was just a little too much for me. It didn't have any real complexity in the flavours. It was a bit one note, but man, that one note was excellent.

3/4
