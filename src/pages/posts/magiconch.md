# Magiconch / マホーツガイ

Its loose-sleeve like arms are actually hollow eyestalks. It can shoot bubbles and water from its eyestalks with force and catch prey in the current. This method of catching prey looks a bit like telekinesis. Its large hat-like shell protects its soft body.

# Occullusc / マジュツガイ

A strong current is constantly produced from within its eyestalks. When it’s not hunting, it attempts to hide this power, which makes it unable to see. Whenever its hat senses danger via electromagnetic waves, its body completely retreats back into its shell.
