Breath kami are considered symbols of peace and calm. People often pray to them before undertaking a stressful task, like an entrance exam or tournament. By doing deep, measured breathing exercises regularly, you can attract one to live with you. They prefer to possess objects that are soft, like plush toys and cushions. Once enshrined close by, it’s said that it will increase the calming effects of mindful breathing.
