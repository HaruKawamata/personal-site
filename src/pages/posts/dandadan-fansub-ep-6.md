> Aira: あたしって美少女すぎる。

> Aira: I'm the prettiest girl in the world.

Here we can see a bit of a translation trap in the word すぎる. In standard Japanese, this word means “too” when combined with another word, as in “too hot”, “too expensive”, “too short”. However, in colloquial Japanese, it’s used for emphasis — the thing it’s modifying doesn’t have to be “too” something (as if there’s some kind of limit to acceptability), it’s just “very” something. In colloquial Japanese, it’s also commonly used on nouns: e.g. オタクすぎる (mega nerd). Here, Aira is using it on 美少女 (beautiful girl), though we must keep in mind that 美少女 is a very common word and sounds much less clunky than “beautiful girl”. 美少女すぎる is closer to “super beautiful girl” than “too pretty”, but I needed this line to sound more natural. Aira also repeatedly refers to herself as a 美少女 so I wanted a translation I’d be able to fall back on, almost like a title she gives herself, that doesn’t sound weird when repeated over and over. I felt like “I’m the prettiest girl in the world” gets across all the ego and arrogance that 美少女すぎる has, while also sounding natural and working as a personal mantra or title.

> Aira: 悪魔よ！自分の世界に帰れ！

> Aira: Begone demon! To the depths of hell with you!

I had a lot of fun translating Aira’s super dramatic fantasy protagonist register! Here, exaggerating the tone and making it sound cliched and overdramatic is more important than the exact wording. Obviously cliches differ between cultures and languages, so while the Japanese sounds extremely cliched, a direct word-by-word translation would not (“Demon! Return to your own world!”).

I think it’s worth pointing out that almost all of the aliens and urban legend spirits in Dandadan are real legends. This is more obvious to Western audiences on the alien/cryptid side (we all know the Flatwoods Monster, Nessie, etc.), but I doubt many people watching a sub would be familiar with the yokai side. ターボババア (the Highway Hag) and アクロバティックサラサラ (the Silken-Haired Acrobat) are both real urban legends in Japan, and I felt like I should emphasise this fact if possible. For example, when Highway Hag is researching the Silken-Haired Acrobat on Muko’s phone:

> Hag: えええ、赤いワンピースにアクロバチックな動き、サラサラな髪。 \
> Momo: 妖怪のくせにあいつのこと知らないの？ \
> Hag: 三下のルーキは眼中にねえのよ。

> Hag: Let's see... Wears a red dress. Moves like an acrobat. Has long, silky, black hair. \
> Momo: You're a yokai too! Shouldn't you already know about her? \
> Hag: A new urban legend like her? She's nothing to me.

I emphasised the fact that she’s a “new urban legend”, even though this is a slight departure from Highway Hag’s original line.
