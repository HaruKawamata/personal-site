I was poking around in Google Maps the other day and I stumbled upon a sandwich shop in my local area that I had never seen before. And with such high reviews! This was quite shocking to me! I was there within the hour.

The shop was very small. Inside seating consisted of a narrow counter with a few high stools on the wall opposite the service counter, plus one small table nestled in the corner. There were also two small tables outside, but the hill that the shop lies on made these options seem a bit awkward. It didn't look like it was primarily a sandwich shop; more like a suburban takeaway coffee shop.

Even more surprising was the fact that despite the high Google reviews, all the sandwiches seemed to be made in advance and displayed in the glass cabinet. They did look good though, so my partner and I ordered and asked for the sandwiches to be toasted.

My god. Insanely good. The first thing I noticed was the quality of the bread. It was incredibly soft, but also thick and satisfying, and the toasting had been done with a very appropriate amount of butter. The exterior was crispy, but not crunchy. The chicken was also delicious - tender, flavoursome, and the mayo did not overpower the experience. To top off the experience, I think they sprinkled crispy bits of fried bacon fat along the length of the sandwich's cross section. Some mouthfuls were an umami bomb, others a little fresher. An incredible sandwich! I will be back!

4/4
